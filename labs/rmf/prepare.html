01
PREPARE // PHASE OVERVIEW

Build a shared understanding before security decisions are made.

Prepare creates the organizational and system-level context used by every RMF phase that follows.

01 Understand the System

Document the Foxhole Portal purpose, users, business processes, services, and operating environment.

02 Identify Stakeholders

Determine who owns, supports, secures, operates, and makes risk decisions for the system.

03 Establish Governance

Assign responsibilities and establish how the authorization team will manage security work and documentation.

04 Define the Scope

Begin establishing the authorization boundary, system dependencies, interfaces, and included technology.

PREPARE OUTCOME

Blue Fox Defense leaves Prepare with an organized authorization effort, assigned responsibilities, documented system context, an initial authorization boundary, identified information types, and enough information to begin Categorize.
