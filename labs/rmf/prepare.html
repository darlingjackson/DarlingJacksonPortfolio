01 / Phase Overview

Why This Phase Matters

Building a shared understanding before security decisions are made.

The Prepare phase gives the authorization team a clear starting point. Before Blue Fox Defense can categorize the Foxhole Portal or select security controls, the organization must understand what the system does, who is responsible for it, where it operates, and which business processes depend on it.

During this phase, the team documents the organizational context, establishes the authorization approach, identifies key stakeholders, and begins defining the system boundary. These decisions create the foundation used throughout the remaining RMF phases.

Prepare Phase Outcome

Blue Fox Defense leaves the Prepare phase with an organized authorization effort, assigned responsibilities, documented system context, and enough information to begin categorizing the Foxhole Portal.

Prepare Objectives

What the team needed to determine.

The Prepare phase focuses on the people, responsibilities, business needs, technical environment, and authorization strategy supporting the Foxhole Portal.

01

Understand the System

Document the Foxhole Portal purpose, supported business processes, primary users, services, and operating environment.

02

Identify Stakeholders

Determine who owns the system, who supports it, who makes risk decisions, and who is responsible for security and authorization activities.

03

Establish Governance

Assign security responsibilities, define decision authority, and establish how the authorization team will communicate and manage documentation.

04

Define the Scope

Begin documenting the authorization boundary, system dependencies, external services, and technology components included in the effort.

Security and technical personnel worked together to identify which components, services, interfaces, and responsibilities belong inside the authorization boundary.
