01
PREPARE // PHASE OVERVIEW

Build a shared understanding before security decisions are made.

Prepare creates the organizational and system-level context used by every RMF phase that follows.

01 Establish Mission & Stakeholders

Define the business purpose of the Foxhole Portal and identify the stakeholders, owners, security personnel, and decision-makers supporting the authorization effort.

02 Identify Assets & Scope

Identify the system assets, major components, supporting services, interfaces, dependencies, and authorization boundary.

03 Understand Information & Risk

Identify the information handled by the system, understand its life cycle, evaluate initial risk, and establish security and privacy requirements.

04 Align Architecture & Registration

Align the system with enterprise architecture, allocate requirements to the appropriate system components, and establish the system registration record.

PREPARE OUTCOME

Blue Fox Defense leaves Prepare with the Foxhole Portal authorization effort organized, key stakeholders and assets identified, the authorization boundary established, information types and life-cycle activities documented, initial risk and requirements recorded, architecture and requirement allocation understood, and the system registered for authorization tracking.
