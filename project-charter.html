Leadership Overview

Executive Summary

This Project Charter formally establishes the Risk Management Framework authorization effort for the Blue Fox Defense Foxhole Portal.

Foxhole Portal is an internal enterprise web application used by Blue Fox Defense employees to access human resources information, company policies, required training, employee self-service functions, internal announcements, organizational information, and shared documentation.

Blue Fox Defense will prepare Foxhole Portal for an Authorization to Operate by completing the organizational and system-level planning activities required to begin the RMF process. This charter defines the initial project scope, establishes governance, identifies expected Prepare phase deliverables, and authorizes the project team to begin the effort.

The charter does not establish a security categorization, identify security controls, or document implementation and assessment results. Those activities remain outside the scope of the Prepare phase.

Charter Authority

Purpose

This Project Charter formally establishes the RMF Prepare phase for the Foxhole Portal authorization effort. It documents the reason for initiating the project, identifies the expected outcomes, defines the initial scope, and establishes the governance structure under which Prepare phase activities will be performed.

Approval of this charter provides the project team with organizational direction to coordinate stakeholders, collect system information, define the authorization approach, and develop the supporting Prepare phase documentation.

Charter Authority

This document authorizes planning and documentation activities. It does not constitute an authorization decision or permit operational use of the system.

2
System Context

Project Background

Blue Fox Defense operates Foxhole Portal as an internal enterprise web application supporting routine employee and organizational business processes.

The portal provides a centralized location for employees to access:

01 Human resources information
02 Company policies and procedures
03 Required employee training
04 Employee self-service functions
05 Internal announcements
06 Organizational information
07 Internal document repository

Foxhole Portal has been identified as requiring a formal authorization effort under the Blue Fox Defense cybersecurity governance program. The project begins with the RMF Prepare phase to establish the organizational, technical, and administrative foundation required for later lifecycle activities.

3
Authorization Driver

Business Need

Foxhole Portal supports business functions used across Blue Fox Defense and provides employees with access to internal resources and organizational information. A formal authorization effort is needed to ensure that cybersecurity risk is identified, documented, evaluated, and communicated through an established governance process.

Completing the RMF process will provide Blue Fox Defense leadership with the information needed to make a risk-based authorization decision regarding the operation of Foxhole Portal.

G
Governance

Establish accountable oversight for system cybersecurity risk.

R
Risk Visibility

Provide leadership with documented information supporting risk decisions.

A
Authorization

Prepare the system for a formal Authorization to Operate decision.

4
Intended Outcomes

Project Objectives

The objectives of the Foxhole Portal RMF Prepare phase are to:

  1. 01 Formally initiate the Foxhole Portal authorization effort.
  2. 02 Establish executive sponsorship and project governance.
  3. 03 Identify participating stakeholders and responsible organizational functions.
  4. 04 Develop an accurate initial description of the system and its operating environment.
  5. 05 Define the proposed authorization boundary and supporting authorization strategy.
  6. 06 Identify information types processed, stored, or transmitted by the system.
  7. 07 Produce the planning artifacts needed to conclude the Prepare phase.

Oversight Structure

Project Governance

The Foxhole Portal authorization effort will be conducted under the direction of the System Owner. Cybersecurity governance and RMF oversight will be provided by the Information System Security Manager. Day-to-day RMF coordination will be performed by the Information System Security Officer with technical support from Systems Engineering and designated subject matter experts.

Executive Direction System Owner

Provides sponsorship, resources, direction, and approval.

Security Oversight ISSM

Oversees cybersecurity governance and RMF alignment.

RMF Coordination ISSO

Coordinates activities, records, and document reviews.

Technical Support Systems Engineering

Develops and validates technical documentation.

Detailed stakeholder information is maintained in BFD-PRE-002, Stakeholder Register. Responsibility assignments are maintained in BFD-PRE-006, Responsibility Matrix.

7
Planning Basis

Assumptions

  • Executive sponsorship will remain available throughout the Prepare phase.
  • Required stakeholders and subject matter experts will participate in scheduled planning and review activities.
  • Existing technical and business documentation will be made available to the project team.
  • Information provided by business owners and technical personnel accurately represents the current operating environment.
  • No major system redesign is expected during the Prepare phase.
