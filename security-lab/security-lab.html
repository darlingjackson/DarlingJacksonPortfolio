The Case Study

Showing what the RMF process looks like when it is applied to an enterprise system.

I created this lab to show what the Risk Management Framework looks like beyond the documentation. Instead of only describing each phase, I built a fictional organization called Blue Fox Defense and a fictional enterprise system called Foxhole Portal.

The lab shows how the decisions, meetings, artifacts, and security activities work together to move a system toward authorization. You can follow the process from beginning to end or jump directly to any phase using the navigation above.

01 / The Organization

Blue Fox Defense

A fictional organization built to support a realistic enterprise security scenario.

Blue Fox Defense is a fictional defense technology company created for this Enterprise Security Compliance Lab. The company provides the environment used to document the authorization of the Foxhole Portal.

Blue Fox Defense is preparing the portal for deployment. Before the system can move forward, the organization must identify the system requirements, document the security controls, assess the risk, and complete the authorization process.

02 / The System

Foxhole Portal

An internal employee portal supporting everyday business operations.

Foxhole Portal is a secure internal HR system that gives authorized personnel access to employee services, training, policies, HR documents, and approved company resources.

Throughout this case study, I will examine the portal as an enterprise information system and work through the security, documentation, and risk decisions required to move it through the RMF lifecycle.

The Portal Supports

  • HR resources
  • Required training
  • Internal policies
  • Employee services
  • Company announcements
  • Internal documentation
